How to use
  1. Start the camera and enable Mouse Mode. Show one hand with your thumb and index finger apart.
  2. Point with your index finger. The guide box maps to your primary screen.
  3. Pinch your thumb and index finger once, then open them to left-click.
  4. Pinch your thumb and middle finger, keeping your index finger extended. Release to right-click.
  5. Pinch your thumb and ring finger, then release to double-click. You can also pinch your index finger twice quickly.
  6. Hold the thumb–index pinch and move your hand to drag. Release to drop.
  7. Raise your index and middle fingers in a V with the other fingers curled. Move your hand up or down to scroll.
  8. Turn on Floating keyboard in Mouse Mode, then select a text field. Touch both index fingertips together for half a second. Point and pinch with one hand to type, or select × to close the keyboard.
  9. To move the keyboard, point at its top bar, hold a thumb–index pinch, and move your hand. Release to place it.
  10. Hold an open palm still to pause. Lower it, then show another steady open palm to resume.
  11. Lower your hand to pause or press Esc to turn Mouse Mode off.
  12. Minimize the window to send AirDesk to the notification area. Click its tray icon to restore it, or right-click to exit.
